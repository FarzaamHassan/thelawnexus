About The Law Nexus

The Law Nexus (Advocates & Corporate Legal Consultants) is a Pakistani corporate law firm founded by Ahmad Hassan, Advocate. We specialise in cross-border work for foreign enterprises entering or operating in Pakistan, with particular depth in Sino-Pakistani transactions. Supported by associated senior counsel and chartered accountants, we deliver integrated legal, regulatory and tax solutions.
